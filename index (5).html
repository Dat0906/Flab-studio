FLAB

New Media Art

Code, light, sound and data, built into places you can walk through.

New media art begins where the interface stops being a tool and becomes the subject. At FLAB we write code the way others carve stone, and we tune light, sound and data until they behave like weather. Our work is made to be walked through, touched and interrupted. It never finishes the same way twice.

  • Generative systemsRules that grow images no one drew.
  • Audiovisual performanceLive sets where sound and picture share one engine.
  • Interactive installationRooms that respond to bodies, voices and crowds.
  • Machine perceptionCameras and models used as instruments, not filters.
